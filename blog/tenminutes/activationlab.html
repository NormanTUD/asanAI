The bend makes depth meaningful.

A dense layer can only stretch, rotate, and shift. The activation function after it is what lets a network learn curves, folds, and decisions.

01 The gate after the projection

A nonlinearity per coordinate.

A dense layer computes an affine map, Ax + b. On its own it maps straight lines to straight lines. A nonlinear activation is applied to each coordinate after the projection and breaks that linearity.

ReLU passes positive values and sends everything negative to zero. Sigmoid and tanh squash the output into a bounded range. GELU is a smooth variant used in many transformers.

02 Why the choice matters

Gradients and probabilities.

The activation decides how information flows and how gradients behave. ReLU is cheap but can produce dead units; sigmoid and tanh saturate; GELU is smooth and works well in deep transformers.

At the output, softmax turns a vector of scores into a probability distribution. It is not just a normalisation: it is the gate that turns the model's internal scores into a choice over the vocabulary.
