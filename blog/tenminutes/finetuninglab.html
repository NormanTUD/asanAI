The assistant is made after pretraining.

A pretrained model is a very good internet simulator. It does not yet follow instructions, refuse harmful requests, or hold a conversation. Post-training is how it becomes an assistant.

01 From raw text to instructions

Teaching the format.

Pretraining teaches next-token prediction on a huge corpus. The result is fluent but uncontrolled. Supervised fine-tuning then shows the model examples of instructions and good answers, teaching it the format of a conversation.

Fine-tuning is not only about facts. It changes behaviour: when to answer, when to refuse, how to structure a response, and which tone to use.

02 Preferences and reasoning

Aligning behaviour.

Preference optimization goes further. Humans (or a model) compare answers, and the model is trained to prefer the better one. RLHF uses a reward model; DPO-style methods learn directly from preference pairs.

Reasoning models add another stage: reinforcement learning on problems with verifiable answers, so the model learns to think longer and check its work. The total post-training cost is small compared with pretraining, but it shapes most of what users experience.
