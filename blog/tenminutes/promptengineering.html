Prompting is communication.

You are not programming the model; you are creating a linguistic environment. Every role, example, and constraint narrows the space of plausible answers.

01 Set the environment

Role, specificity, structure.

A role primes the model with a style and a body of knowledge. Specific instructions reduce ambiguity. Delimiters separate instructions from data. Examples show the format you want better than a description often can.

Negations are weak: telling a model not to think of a white bear can make the bear more likely. Say what you want, not only what you do not want.

02 Keep it lean and testable

Iterate, do not decorate.

Long prompts are not automatically better. Every irrelevant sentence adds noise and costs tokens. Canonicalise the input, remove boilerplate, and make the task easy to verify.

Prompting is empirical. Change one thing, test it, and keep what works. A prompt that is clear to a human is usually a good starting point for a model.
