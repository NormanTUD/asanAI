The social effects are already here.

AI is not only a technical project. It changes how images are made, how work is organised, how news is trusted, and how people imagine the future.

01 A short history of expectations

Hope, winter, spring.

The field began with enormous optimism at the Dartmouth workshop in 1956, followed by winters when the promises outran the results. The public relationship has oscillated ever since: hope, disappointment, and another spring.

What is different now is scale. Synthetic images, voices, and text are cheap and abundant. That changes the economics of creation and the cost of deception.

02 Creation, work, and truth

The effects are uneven.

Generative models expand what can be made, but they also flood the world with synthetic media. Voice cloning and deepfakes make fraud and political deception easier. The same tools can help artists, scientists, and students.

The effects on work are uneven. Some tasks are automated; others are augmented; new tasks appear. The important questions are about power, accountability, and who gets to decide how the tools are used.
