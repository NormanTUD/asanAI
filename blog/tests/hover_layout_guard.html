Basic Math Concepts I — The Numerical Foundations

Before we can understand a neural network, we have to understand the language it speaks: mathematics. This first of three math chapters covers the numerical foundations, the symbols and operations that appear constantly in AI code. The attention mechanism and each embedding of a token are introduced before the first gradient step, so the reader always knows what every symbol means the moment it is written down for the first time in this course.

By the end, you will be comfortable with the sum symbol, Euler's number, logarithms, and the way a computer represents infinity without crashing. None of this requires a physics degree; it requires care, and the willingness to write the same equation three times until it stops being magic.
