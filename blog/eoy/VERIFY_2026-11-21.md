# EOY Intuition — independent claim check

Date: 2026-11-21
Scope: `blog/eoy/data.js` (German and English), every active scene in
`blog/eoy/demos/`, and the scenes in `blog/eoy/assets/learnsys-*.js`,
`assets/tf-sinus-scene.js`, `assets/loss-landscape-scene.js`,
`assets/space-morph.js`. `demos/archive/`, `demos/code.js`,
`demos/phone.js` and `assets/demos.js` are not loaded by the EOY app and were
excluded.

Method: every checkable sentence was extracted from `body`, `teaser`, `watch`,
`context`, `deep`, boxes, questions and source notes; every numeric, date,
model-size, token-count, attribution and formula claim was compared with the
primary paper or book named in the lesson. Demo data was checked by executing
or re-implementing the demo logic (BPE, Othello legality, softmax, cross-entropy,
token IDs, sinus data, death years). Every `url:` in `data.js` and every demo
source URL was requested; arXiv metadata and Crossref/OpenAlex records were
used where a publisher blocked automated access.

## Result

- 31 steps, 61 substeps, 38 active scenes.
- 116 unique HTTP `data.js` source URLs: 108 returned HTTP 200; 8 returned
  publisher/bot blocks (seven 403 and one 202) and were checked through
  Crossref, OpenAlex or the arXiv record instead. No dead link was found.
  The `../test/attention_as_graph.html` link is a local relative link and
  resolves to an existing file.
- 15 confirmed errors or overstatements were corrected in this pass
  (table below).
- No remaining known false numeric, date or attribution claim.
- 10 open items are marked `⚠` or `❓` below: they are time-sensitive,
  deliberately didactic, or not independently verifiable from the named
  source. None is presented as a hard fact without a caveat after the fixes.
