⚠️ Why the LLM numbers are a loose analogy, not a literal bound. All of this is exact only for pure ReLU (piecewise-linear) nets. Modern LLMs differ, so read those presets as a sense of scale: their feed-forward block uses GELU (GPT) or SwiGLU / SiLU (LLaMA) — smooth, not piecewise-linear — so there are no crisp "linear regions" to count; and they interleave attention (softmax) and normalization, which are not dense ReLU blocks either. The exponential-in-depth intuition carries over; the exact slice count does not.
