About the four layers

Every paper carries four layers, written so that the same idea reaches you at whatever altitude you're standing on today.

Wave 1 (31): foundational papers in AI-adjacent fields. Wave 2 (62): broader categories — ancient studies, theology, anthropology, architecture, music, film, astronomy, geology, chemistry, medicine. Wave 3 (51): truly universal ideas — one sentence unlocks thousands. Wave 4 (48): AI and neural networks in depth. Wave 5 (25): the originals — forgotten founders, pre-prints, original equations. Wave 6 (22): hidden foundations — pre-modern originals. Wave 7 (25): forgotten first authors — papers that already had the answer decades before the field got credit (Cauchy, de Moivre, Laplace, Linnainmaa, Ivakhnenko). Wave 8 (25): the untranslated originals — Soviet preprints, Italian doctoral theses, UK Ministry reports, French posthumous books (Lyapunov 1892, Monge 1781, Kotelnikov 1933, Manin 1980, Wiesner 1983). Default: Wave 8.

  • Elementary — one vivid sentence, no jargon, true to the heart of the idea.
  • ⚡ Aha — the counter-intuitive flash that connects the paper to your life today.
  • Abitur — what a sharp A-level / Abitur student should walk away with after a thoughtful reading.
  • PhD — the precise claim, the technical apparatus, and the non-obvious consequence.
