About the four layers

Every paper carries four layers, written so that the same idea reaches you at whatever altitude you're standing on today.

Wave 1 (31): foundational papers in AI-adjacent fields. Wave 2 (62): broader categories — ancient studies, theology, anthropology, architecture, music, film, astronomy, geology, chemistry, medicine. Wave 3 (51): truly universal ideas — one sentence unlocks thousands. Wave 4 (48): AI and neural networks in depth. Default: Wave 4.

  • Elementary — one vivid sentence, no jargon, true to the heart of the idea.
  • ⚡ Aha — the counter-intuitive flash that connects the paper to your life today.
  • Abitur — what a sharp A-level / Abitur student should walk away with after a thoughtful reading.
  • PhD — the precise claim, the technical apparatus, and the non-obvious consequence.

Curated to complement (not duplicate) the asanAI course. PDFs link to public archives where possible; some links point to publisher pages where the original is paywalled.
