The decisive question. What commands (heißt) us to think? The most thought-provoking.

χρή τὸ λέγειν τε νοεῖν τ᾿ ἐὸν ἔμμεναι

Parmenides' saying: the command (Geheiß) that first called Western thinking into being. Explore each word interactively.

χρή — "It needs" / "There is a use for"

Not mere necessity or compulsion. From χράω: to handle, to use — in the high sense of letting something into its essence and keeping it there. A sheltering command.

λέγειν — Letting-lie-before

Not "saying" in the vocal sense. From λέγω = to lay, to gather. Saying is a laying-forth: letting what lies before us lie forth as what it is.

νοεῖν — Taking-to-heart

Not "thinking" in the logical sense. From νοῦς: apprehending, heeding, perceiving with the heart-mind. Taking into safe-keeping (Acht).

ἐόν — Present being / what presences

The supreme participle: "being" as both noun and verb. It names the Twofold (Zwiefalt) — beings in their Being, presencing of what presences.

ἔμμεναι — to be / presencing

The infinitive of εἶναι. Presencing as such — the coming-into-presence, the emerging from unconcealment into the open.
