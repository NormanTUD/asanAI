The Foam of Meaning

Meaning has a shape.

Not a label stuck onto a word. A landscape — clusters, filaments, voids — in the space of everything that could be said.

Step 5 · Self-similarity

Zoom in. Same shape.

Clusters, filaments, voids at every magnification. Language has no preferred scale.

Step 6 · Metaphor

A bridge thrown across the void.

Not a short step inside a cluster — a long-range filament that carries structure from one distant region to another.

Step 7 · Form vs. meaning

Many ways to say one thing.

Each meaning in S is covered by a whole fiber of forms in X.
Paraphrase = moving inside a fiber. Synonymy is a property of fibers, not of points.

Step 8 · The consistency law

Both roads reach the same meaning.

π′∘f = g∘π — transform the form then interpret, or interpret then transform the meaning.
That agreement is compositionality.
