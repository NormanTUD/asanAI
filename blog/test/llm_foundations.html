The atoms of an LLM.

Everything a transformer does is built from a handful of typed operations. Not layers, not attention, not “reasoning”: just scalars, vectors, pairings, linear maps, one nonlinearity, softmax, addition, composition, a loss, a gradient and an update. This page builds the whole thing from those primitives, one rule at a time.
