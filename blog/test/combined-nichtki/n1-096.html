Coherence Is Local Gluing

Fusion aus: hott_book.html ✕ coherent_worldview.html

Kernthese der Fusion

Both papers, in different idioms, defend a single thesis: a coherent whole is not imposed from outside — it emerges when local pieces agree on their overlaps — and the "sameness" that lets them agree is itself a structured, higher-dimensional object, not a primitive. One paper states it as a foundation of mathematics; the other states it as an epistemology of how a mind assembles a worldview.

A · HoTT HoTT says it mathematically: types are spaces, elements are points, and identifications are paths between points — and paths between paths, ad infinitum (an ∞-groupoid). The right notion of sameness between types is equivalence, and the univalence axiom makes equivalence literally equality: (A = B) ≃ (A ≃ B). Structure is local (fibres over points, transport along paths) and glues to the whole uniquely, by the fundamental theorem of identity types: a family B(x) characterizes the identity type of A iff its total space Σ(x:A) B(x) is contractible.

B · COHERENT COHERENT says it epistemically: a worldview G is a coherent global whole obtained from local readings R_i of the world W that agree on their overlaps (a cover / sheaf glued into a section). Its nine-rung chain runs Distinction → Relation → Transformation → Locality → Compatibility → Coherence → Gluing → Globality → Invariance, and its coda holds that the whole holds because of the fitting, not sameness. Truth is a 2×2 of coherence (internal consistency) × correspondence (contact with W), and five pathologies — fantasy, factbook, liar, calibrated, out-of-cover — are the ways the local pieces fail to glue.

Fused: HoTT is the mathematics of the gluing (transport, contractibility, univalence); COHERENT is the epistemology (world → readings → whole, truth as coherence × correspondence, the failure modes). Taken together: a coherent worldview is a type in a universe, and it is true exactly when it is equivalent — hence, by univalence, equal — to the structure of the world it reads.

Widersprüche zwischen A und B

C1 · Where does "equivalence" sit relative to "equality"?

A HoTT promotes equivalence to the top. Univalence asserts (A = B) ≃ (A ≃ B): for types in the universe 𝒰, "equivalent" is "equal" — the identity type of types is the equivalence type. HoTT also separates judgmental/definitional equality ≡ from identity types = (paths), and notes that has-inverse(f) is "homotopically complex" while is-equiv(f) is "always a proposition (has at most one proof)."

B COHERENT keeps a sameness ladder with equality strictly above equivalence. Its ladder is = (identity) at the very top, then ≅ (isomorphism), ≃ (equivalence), ≈ (approximation), statistical, model-theoretic — and its animation refuses a weaker relation (≈) when it tries to drag up to =. Here = is strictly stronger than ≃.

Conflict: A identifies the top two rungs (equivalence = equality in the universe); B keeps them apart and forbids promoting a weak sameness to identity.

C2 · Is coherence guaranteed, or fragile?

A HoTT: coherence is guaranteed by construction. By the fundamental theorem of identity types / encode–decode, a family B(x) characterizes A's identity type iff Σ(x:A) B(x) is contractible; contractibility makes the gluing unique (at most one path). Give the local data and the global is forced — "coherence by definition."

B COHERENT: coherence is a fragile achievement that can and does fail. The whole G is a separate target the local readings must successfully glue to produce; the five pathologies are exactly the failure modes (factbook = no global section; fantasy = coherent but untethered; liar = source untethered; calibrated = small bias; out-of-cover).

Conflict: A's glue is automatic once the condition holds; B's glue is a risk you must verify. "Guaranteed" vs. "can fail."

C3 · Is there an external world the whole must correspond to?

B COHERENT: yes, strongly. Truth = coherence × correspondence. Coherence without contact with the world W is "fantasy" (a self-consistent but untethered whole). And W is only partially accessible — the "blind-spot ring" (accessible cover c_acc ⊂ W). A true worldview must be self-consistent and touch the outside, with bounded access.

A HoTT: no external world. Everything is internal to the universe 𝒰; correctness is internal coherence (a proof is a path in a type; univalence is a principle about 𝒰). There is no independent W the theory must "contact" — 𝒰 is its own world, and "correspondence" is internalized as equivalence.

Conflict: B demands an external correspondence term and a bounded-access caveat that A has no counterpart for; in A a coherent structure is correct by construction, with no "fantasy" failure mode.

Auflösung

R1 (for C1) · Stratify: truncation levels are the sameness ladder

HoTT's truncation hierarchy — level −2 contractible, −1 proposition, 0 set, 1 groupoid, …, ∞-type — is a precise ladder of how much higher-path structure a type carries. COHERENT's epistemic ladder (=, ≅, ≃, ≈) is the same idea from the reader's side. Fused: the truncation level of a structure determines the highest rung of sameness it can honestly support. A set (level 0) is "discrete": its identity type is a proposition, so for a set ≃ and = coincide and there is no room to climb — which is exactly B's refusal (≈ cannot reach =). The universe 𝒰 is a high ∞-type, so on 𝒰's own rung the top two rungs collapse — which is exactly A's univalence. No contradiction: the same statement read at different truncation levels.

R2 (for C2) · The condition and its failure modes are one phenomenon

A supplies the condition for gluing (B(x) has contractible total space ⟺ the local readings agree on overlaps, unique transport); B enumerates the ways that condition fails. The five pathologies are the non-compatibility failures in HoTT clothing: factbook (true local dots, no global section) = a cover with no global section; fantasy (coherent, no W-contact) = a family contractible over the wrong base; liar (untethered source) = local readings that do not descend from W, so their "paths" carry no real transport; calibrated (small bias) = transport that is only approximately coherent (small holonomy); out-of-cover = querying a point outside the base space, where transport is undefined. Fused: coherence is guaranteed given the compatibility condition (A), and the pathologies are the check for that condition (B). Encode–decode is the algorithm; the five pathologies are the error messages. "By construction" vs. "can fail" reconcile as sufficiency vs. verification.

R3 (for C3) · Internalize the world, or keep it as a meta-anchor

Two readings, both valid. Internal: B's "world W" is A's universe 𝒰; a "reading R_i" is a structure inside 𝒰; "contact with the world" = equivalence to the corresponding structure in 𝒰, which by univalence is equality. "Fantasy" (coherent but untethered) = a self-consistent structure that is not equivalent to any real structure in 𝒰 — the wrong shape. Applied / external: for B's LLM (corpus-as-cover), history, and embodiment ladder there is a genuinely external empirical W no self-contained theory reaches; then A supplies the internal logic and B supplies the external anchor. Fused truth criterion: a worldview is a coherent type in 𝒰, true iff it is equivalent to the world's own type; univalence says that equivalence is equality in the universe. So B's 2×2 (coherence × correspondence) is A's univalence — coherence = being a well-formed, self-consistent ∞-groupoid; correspondence = equivalence to W's type; univalence = the move that makes "corresponds to the world" the same as "is the world (structurally)." The blind-spot ring = you may assert equivalence only on the part of W you can see; outside your accessible cover, transport is undefined (out-of-cover).

Fusionierter Inhalt

The two papers share a vocabulary once you read HoTT's machinery as the formal engine under COHERENT's epistemes. The fused content is the union of the two, with a mapping between them.

From A (HoTT) — the mathematics of gluing

  • Types, elements, judgments. A type is a space; Γ ⊢ a : A is a judgment. A type family B : A → 𝒰 is a fibre bundle (a fibre B(x) over each point x).
  • Π-types as sections. A dependent function f : Π(x:A) B(x) is a section of the bundle — one point per fibre; with formation / introduction (λ) / elimination (eval) / computation (β+η).
  • Inductive types. ℕ = 0, succ. The trap: 0 + n = n needs a proof (an identification), not definitional equality.
  • Identity types = paths. a =_A x is the space of paths; an ∞-groupoid (refl, inverse, concatenation, associativity, unit/inverse laws); transport tr_B(p) : B(x) → B(y) moves fibres along paths.
  • Equivalences & fibres. A bi-invertible map; fib_f(b) = Σ(a:A) f(a)=b; f is an equivalence ⟺ all its fibres are contractible.
  • Contractibility & the fundamental fact. Σ(x:A) a = x is contractible at (a, refl) — the basis for characterizing an identity type via a contractible total space (encode–decode).
  • Truncation levels. −2 contractible, −1 proposition, 0 set, 1 groupoid, k-type, ∞. (∅,𝟏 props; ℕ,ℤ,Bool sets; 𝒰 not a set; S¹ a 1-groupoid with loop space ℤ.)
  • Univalence. idtoeqv : (A = B) → (A ≃ B) is an equivalence; ua : (A ≃ B) → (A = B); transport(ua(f), x) = f(x). Consequences: structure-identity principle, function extensionality, 𝒰 not a set.
  • Higher inductive types. S¹ = base, loop (loop ≠ refl); π₁(S¹) ≅ ℤ; universal cover = the helix ℝ → S¹; encode–decode; suspensions, pushouts, truncations, quotients.

From B (COHERENT) — the epistemology of a coherent whole

  • The nine-rung chain: Distinction, Relation, Transformation, Locality, Compatibility, Coherence, Gluing, Globality, Invariance.
  • Locality → Sheaf. Local readings (nested open sets / a panorama of overlapping frames) must agree on every overlap; a global section exists iff they do. Misalign one frame and the glue breaks.
  • Master diagram W → R_i → G with a blind-spot ring (c_acc ⊂ W): the world, its readings, the coherent global whole.
  • Three kinds of difference: World (W), Instrument (I), Interpretation (J) — two disagreeing reports traced to different sources of difference.
  • Sameness ladder: = > ≅ > ≃ > ≈ > statistical > model-theoretic; a weaker rung cannot be promoted to identity.
  • Admissible transitions (licence pool T): widen T and spurious fits appear — a model-selection / Occam principle.
  • Truth 2×2 (coherence × correspondence): good (coherent ∧ contact), factbook (incoherent ∧ contact), fantasy (coherent ∧ no contact), wrong (incoherent ∧ no contact).
  • Five pathologies: fantasy, factbook, liar, calibrated, out-of-cover.
  • LLM as corpus-as-cover: the corpus is an open cover of the world; training glues the local documents into a global model G; compounding feeds G on its own output; there is no direct W → G arrow.
  • Embodiment ladder: grammar → topos → manifold → ℝᵈ → floats → silicon (each descent loses generality, gains causality). History constellation: Parmenides → … → Grothendieck. Coda: the seams stay visible — the whole holds because of the fitting, not sameness.

A cover of eight overlapping local readings R_i on the circle S¹ (the base space). The universal-cover helix ℝ→S¹ lifts the circle into sheets: a global section (the coherent whole G) exists iff the total winding W + δ is an integer. The sameness ladder falls from = to ≈ as the twist grows; the univalence toggle promotes ≃ up to = when the seam is closed.
