The Transformer as a Situs: a Coverage-Conditioned Interpolator

Fusion aus: paperidea4.html ✕ understanding_deep_learning.html

paperidea4.html (the Situs) reads a transformer as a sheaf on a topological site: embedding = coarsest global section, attention = selecting a covering, FFN = local-geometry expansion, hallucination = where the covering is thin. understanding_deep_learning.html (Prince's Understanding Deep Learning) gives the honest functional account and is candid that "why does deep learning work / generalize" is an open mystery. Fused position: the Situs picture is a candidate geometric interpretation that answers - inside its own assumptions - the questions B leaves open; coverage is the hinge where B's success (generalization) and B's failure (hallucination) split.

01

Kernthese der Fusion

B (Understanding Deep Learning) is the functional/operational account: a network is "simply a mathematical function that maps inputs to outputs"; ReLU units make it piecewise-linear; depth folds the input into more linear regions; gradient descent minimizes a maximum-likelihood loss; backprop is the chain rule made linear by reuse; self-attention is a "dynamic, content-dependent wiring pattern". It is honest at the frontier - "Understanding why models generalize (or don't) is one of the deepest open questions in deep learning", "This shouldn't work - but it does" - ending on unresolved mysteries (benign loss landscape, implicit regularization, lottery tickets, grokking).

A (The Situs) is a geometric interpretation of that same transformer: knowledge "as a sheaf on a topological site", resolved from blurry global overview to sharp local detail. The embedding is "the space of global sections - the coarsest image of the situs" (holographic: "every piece contains the whole image, just at lower resolution"); attention "selects a covering: which stalks to resolve" (each head a lens, trading resolution against breadth); the FFN "locally expands geometry ... so information is encoded in the Jacobian field"; the pipeline is Route (words→words) → Resolve (concepts→concepts) → Glue (a readable answer). Hallucination is located precisely: "the model confidently expands a region never properly learned - filling it with topologically plausible but factually wrong continuations" (high confidence, low coverage).

One integrated position. A's Situs picture is not a rival to B's account but a candidate geometric interpretation of it, and it puts one testable variable at the center: coverage - how thick the data's support / the covering is at a region. The fused claim: a transformer is a coverage-conditioned interpolator - a sheaf of local, piecewise-geometric readings over a site whose coverings attention chooses and whose depth runs coarse→fine. Where the covering is thick, B's smooth interpolation is good generalization (double descent); where it is thin, that very same smooth, confident continuation is A's hallucination. B's "open mystery" is exactly the coverage structure A's geometry makes visible: B is the problem, A is one answer, coverage is where they meet.

02

Widersprüche zwischen A und B

No contradiction in goal, but incompatible stances at four points - what each actually says:

Widerspruch 1 · Status of the explanation ("we have the theory" vs. "nobody knows")

Bdeclares the mechanism an open problem: "Understanding why models generalize (or don't) is one of the deepest open questions in deep learning"; "This shouldn't work - but it does". Aasserts a concrete mechanism: knowledge "as a sheaf on a topological site" (the "Grothendieck situs conjecture") - embedding = global sections, attention = selecting coverings, FFN = local geometry, final = gluing. Incompatible: B says the how is unknown; A says it is explained (as a conjecture).

Widerspruch 2 · What attention does (routing vs. focusing)

Bself-attention is "a mechanism where the connections between words depend on the words themselves" - a "dynamic, content-dependent wiring pattern" ("'it' can attend to 'restaurant'"); a functional routing story (Q/K/V, softmax). Aattention "selects a covering: which stalks to resolve"; each head is a "lens"; the point is a resolution–breadth tradeoff - a geometric focusing story. Incompatible framing of the same operator: data-dependent routing (B) vs. covering-selection / detail focusing (A).

Widerspruch 3 · What depth buys (regions vs. resolution)

Bdepth = composing functions → "exponentially more linear regions" N_regions ≤ (D+1)^K; depth is expressive power. Adepth = progressive scale/resolution: route (words→words) → resolve (concepts→concepts) → glue; a zoom coarse→fine, not a region count. Incompatible accounts of "why go deep": counting pieces (B) vs. tracking a resolution scale (A).

Widerspruch 4 · Interpolation: the virtue vs. the vice

B"Over-parameterized models interpolate smoothly between data points, which generalizes well" (test error drops again past the interpolation threshold). Smooth interpolation = the mechanism of success. A"the model confidently expands a region never properly learned - filling it with topologically plausible but factually wrong continuations". Smooth confident extrapolation across thin coverage = the mechanism of hallucination. Incompatible valence for the same property.

Ehrliche EinordnungInterpretive tensions at the seam of two registers - a conjectural geometric ontology (A) vs. an honest empirical textbook (B) - not a formal contradiction. Sharpest: #1 (open problem vs. proposed solution) and #4 (one property, opposite valence); #2/#3 are the same quantity read at different levels.
03

Auflösung

A and B are problem and candidate answer, not rivals:

R1 → W1: B names the question, A proposes one answer

Keep B's honest "no settled theory" framing and read A's Situs picture as a checkable interpretation of the same machinery. The Situs conjecture is a hypothesis about the geometry of the transformer B describes; it answers B's "why" not as a theorem but as a legible picture. Fusion: B = the open problem; A = a candidate geometric answer; B's mysteries (generalization, hallucination) are the acceptance tests A must pass. They differ in altitude, not in target.

R2 → W2: routing is covering-selection

Attention's data-dependent wiring (B) is the selection of which patches/stalks get detail (A): where a head points = which tokens get high resolution = a cover of the site. The resolution–breadth tradeoff (A) is the resource constraint behind B's softmax-normalized weights. Fusion: attention = routing (B) = covering-selection / lens (A); the softmax budget is the resolution budget.

R3 → W3: regions and resolution are two views of one composition

Complementary: depth multiplies linear regions (B) and progresses coarse→fine (A). The folded/composed space B builds is the domain over which A's sheaf resolves local→global. Fusion: depth = (D+1)^K pieces (B) = routing → resolution → gluing (A) over the same folded space.

R4 → W4: the missing variable is coverage

The property B praises and A warns about is the same smooth, confident continuation; its valence is set by coverage. Within the support: smooth interpolation = good generalization (B's double descent). Outside it: the same continuation = hallucination (A). Fusion: interpolation is good where covered, hallucinatory where uncovered; B's "double descent" is the aggregate shadow of A's local coverage/resolution tradeoff.

Fusionierte Schlussfolgerung

A transformer is a coverage-conditioned interpolator: a sheaf of local piecewise-geometric readings over a site, attention choosing the coverings, depth moving coarse→fine. It generalizes where the covering is thick (B's success) and hallucinates where it is thin (A's failure). B's "open mystery" is precisely the coverage structure A's geometry makes visible - coverage is the single variable the whole fusion turns on.

04

Fusionierter Inhalt

The operational core (B - Understanding Deep Learning)

  • Network = function, ReLU = joint. A shallow ReLU net is piecewise-linear: y = φ_0 + ∑ φd · ReLU[θd0 + θd1 x]; each hidden unit adds a "joint". Universal Approximation: enough units → any continuous function; "enough" may be impractical → go deep. ReLU's derivative is 0 or 1 → clean gradients, no vanishing.
  • Depth = exponential regions. K layers of D units → N_regions ≤ (D+1)^K (the "folding" metaphor) - depth efficiency.
  • Training + backprop. φ_{t+1} ← φ_t - α · ∂L/∂φ (LR dilemma; Adam adapts). Backprop = the chain rule: ∂ℓ/∂f_{k-1} = I[f_{k-1}>0] ⊙ (Ωk^T ∂ℓ/∂f_k) - each weight scaled by its source activation; reuse makes it linear in size.
  • Loss = maximum likelihood. φ&^ = argmin [−∑ log Pr(y_i | f[x_i,φ])]: Gaussian → least squares, Bernoulli → cross-entropy. Recipe: pick a distribution, predict its parameters, minimize the negative log-likelihood.
  • Self-attention. Sa[X] = V · softmax[K^T Q / √D_q]; "the connections between words depend on the words themselves" (dynamic, content-dependent wiring).
  • Generalization mystery + double descent. Test error is U-shaped then drops again past the interpolation threshold; over-parameterized models "interpolate smoothly ... which generalizes well". "Nobody fully understands why." Generative: GANs (a two-network game), diffusion (add then reverse noise). Open mysteries: benign loss landscape, implicit regularization (flat minima), lottery tickets, grokking.

The geometric reading (A - The Situs)

  • Situs / sheaf: the LM's world-knowledge is "a sheaf on a topological site"; the input context is the base over which it lives (conjecture).
  • Embedding = global sections = coarsest image. Holographic: "every piece contains the whole image, just at lower resolution"; damage degrades it gracefully.
  • Attention = selecting a covering (which stalks to resolve); heads are lenses; the resolution–breadth tradeoff is a budget split across heads. FFN = expanding local geometry via the Jacobian field (weight centers interfere; d_ff ≈ 4d, e.g. 768→~3072).
  • Pipeline = Route → Resolve → Glue. Routers ("connect words to words", "determine where to look") → resolvers ("connect concepts to concepts", finer) → glues the patches into a readable answer.
  • Hallucination = confident expansion across uncovered patches. "Confidence stays high even where knowledge is thin" - plausible but wrong fill where training thins.

One object, two levels
