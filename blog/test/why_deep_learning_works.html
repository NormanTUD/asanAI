Why Deep Learning Works

A single neuron can only draw one straight line. But stack many together, and something magical happens — the network learns to bend space itself until impossible problems become trivially separable.

"On its own, one layer is powerless — it can only mix, rotate, and scale. But chained together, simple steps stack into arbitrarily complex ones." [[1]]
