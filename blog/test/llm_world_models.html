A visual essay

The World
Inside the Model

How next-token prediction builds a map of the world, and how attention and MLPs read it.

Scroll to begin

A dark, animated visual essay about world models inside large language models.

01 · The substrate

A river of meaning

One stream per token. Attention moves information between streams; MLPs transform it in place.

After Elhage et al., A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits, 2021.

Animation: token columns connected by attention arcs, feeding a central residual stream where MLP nodes add vectors.

02 · Two kinds of dream

One plans. One predicts.

MuZero builds a model to imagine. An LLM builds one as a side effect of next-token prediction.

Ha & Schmidhuber, World Models, 2018 · Schrittwieser et al., MuZero, 2020.

Split animation: left, a learned model rolling out imagined futures; right, a transformer stack where a world state emerges from token prediction.

03 · The board appears

It never saw a board.

Only moves. Yet the board state appears in the activations.

Li et al., Emergent World Representations, ICLR 2023 · Nanda, Othello-GPT is linear, 2023.

Animation: a stream of Othello moves on the left; on the right, discs appear and flip on a board; a probe lens sweeps over the board, revealing a heat map of the model's internal board state.

04 · The representation

A direction, not a table.

Each square is a coordinate: mine ↔ theirs.

Nanda, Lee & Wattenberg, Emergent Linear Representations, 2023 · Park et al., The Linear Representation Hypothesis, 2024.

Animation: a three-dimensional point cloud of activation directions, with one highlighted vector projected onto a glowing mine-versus-theirs axis; a tile card cycles between empty, mine and theirs.

05 · The intervention

Change the map. Change the move.

Edit the internal state, and the model plays a different board.

Li et al., 2023 · Nanda et al., 2023 · Geiger et al., Causal Abstractions, 2021.

Animation: a board with legal moves, a latent activation pushed along the mine-theirs direction, and a bar chart of next-move probabilities morphing after the internal board is edited.

06 · The hidden variable

It learns the board. And the player.

Chess forces a second latent: how strong the game is.

Karvonen, Emergent World Models and Latent Variable Estimation in Chess-Playing Language Models, COLM 2024.

Animation: a chessboard where an internal pawn is deleted, a skill gauge rises, and a win-rate bar grows from 16.7 to 43.2 percent.

07 · The world map

A map of the world, written in activations.

Latitude, longitude, and time are linear directions.

Gurnee & Tegmark, Language Models Represent Space and Time, ICLR 2024.

Animation: a rotating holographic globe with city lights, beside a linear timeline of historical events; a probe scan travels through time.

08 · The geometry

Truth is a line. Weeks are a circle.

Some concepts are directions. Some need a whole subspace.

Marks & Tegmark, The Geometry of Truth, 2024 · Engels et al., Not All Features Are One-Dimensionally Linear, 2025.

Animation: true and false statements separate along a glowing line; beside it, concentric circles for days of the week and months of the year compute modular arithmetic.

09 · The readout

The map is read by circuits.

Attention routes. MLPs remember. The residual stream carries.

Olsson et al., In-context Learning and Induction Heads, 2022 · Geva et al., FFN Layers Are Key-Value Memories, 2021 · Meng et al., ROME, 2022.

Split animation: left, an induction-head circuit copying a pattern across a token sequence; right, an MLP key-value memory recalling Seattle and being edited to Paris.

10 · The synthesis

A map, not the territory.

Compressed, editable, plural, and sometimes wrong.

Nanda, 2023 · Elhage et al., Toy Models of Superposition, 2022 · Engels et al., 2025.

Animation: a floating holographic map above a dark mountain landscape; some tiles flicker and one beam reveals only a small patch of the terrain.
