A dark, animated visual essay about world models inside large language models.

01 · The board appears

It never saw a board.

Only moves. Yet the board state appears in the activations.

Li et al., Emergent World Representations, ICLR 2023 · Nanda, Othello-GPT is linear, 2023.

Animation: a stream of Othello moves on the left; on the right, discs appear and flip on a board; a probe lens sweeps over the board, revealing a heat map of the model's internal board state.

02 · The training sample

It saw moves. It predicted moves.

A real synthetic game. The target is the next move; the model's top five predictions are all legal.

Real forward pass through the public Othello-GPT checkpoint, trained only on next-move prediction.

A real Othello game is shown as input, followed by the model's top eight next-move predictions; the dataset's next move is highlighted in gold. A board shows the true position and legal moves.

03 · The machine

Eight layers. Eight heads. One stream.

The only input is a move token. Attention and MLPs write into a 512-dimensional residual stream.

Othello-GPT: 8 layers, 8 heads, 512 dimensions, 2048-wide MLP, 25M parameters. Li et al., 2023.

A vertical stack of eight transformer layers, each with eight attention heads and an MLP, writing into a shared 512-dimensional residual stream for each token.

04 · Attention

Every head is a softmax over moves.

Real layer-6 attention for the last token. Each row is one head; each column is an earlier move.

Real attention weights from the public checkpoint. Li et al., 2023.

An eight by thirty heatmap of real attention weights: eight heads, thirty earlier moves. Brighter cells mean the last token attends more strongly to that move.

05 · Where it emerges

The board becomes readable by layer four.

A layer-6 probe transferred to each layer's residual stream. Accuracy by layer, on the real activation.

Probe accuracy per layer on the real activation. Probe weights: Nanda et al., 2023.

A bar chart of board-state probe accuracy across the eight layers, rising to one hundred percent by layer four, and a line showing residual-stream norm.

06 · How it builds the board

How the machine builds the board.

No board buffer. Attention copies, MLPs detect, the residual stream accumulates. By layer four, the 64 square directions are readable.

Real layer-by-layer probe readout on the real activation. Probe weights: Nanda et al., 2023.

A board assembles across eight layers: early layers have wrong squares highlighted, layers four to six are fully correct, layer seven degrades slightly. Text explains embedding, attention, MLP and the residual stream.

07 · Where is the board?

The board is not in the weights.

The weights are the machine. The activation is this game’s state. The probe is a separate lens.

Li et al., 2023 · Nanda et al., 2023. Real forward pass through the public Othello-GPT checkpoint. Real linear-probe weights from Nanda et al. The board is the probe readout.

Three columns: the fixed model weights, the 512-dimensional activation for the current game, and the fixed probe matrix. The activation changes between two games; the probe reads out a different board.

08 · The probe

64 × 3 numbers become a board.

Each of the 64 × 3 logits is one dot product. Reshape, softmax, argmax: the board.

Li et al., 2023 · Nanda et al., 2023. Real activation, real probe, real board.

A 64 by 512 probe matrix multiplies a 512-dimensional activation to produce 64 by 3 logits. The logits are reshaped into three 8 by 8 heatmaps and the argmax board.

09 · One square

One square is a direction.

E4 has a 512-dimensional weight vector. The probe is a dot product: empty, white, black.

Nanda et al., 2023 · Park et al., 2024. Real activation and real E4 directions from the public probe.

The E4 probe has three 512-dimensional directions. The activation is multiplied elementwise with the mine direction and summed. The three logits become probabilities and a tile state. A bar shows that 128 of 512 dimensions are used by the board.

10 · The arithmetic

One dot product, then sixty-four.

Same activation, different probe rows. The argmax of each row is a square of the board.

Real layer-6 activation, real mode-0 probe. Full-precision dot products; 64 / 64 squares correct.

A table shows the 512-dimensional dot product for E4 with real numbers, the three logits and softmax probabilities, and the same calculation for four squares, producing the board.

11 · The geometry

A constellation, not a picture.

64 squares × 2 directions = 128 of 512 dimensions. The board is a coordinate system.

Nanda et al., 2023 · Elhage et al., Toy Models of Superposition, 2022. PCA of the real 64 linear-probe directions.

The 64 real probe directions projected to two dimensions form a spread-out constellation, not a board picture. A cosine-similarity heatmap shows most pairs are nearly orthogonal.

12 · The intervention

Change the map. Change the move.

Edit the internal state, and the model plays a different board.

Li et al., 2023 · Nanda et al., 2023 · Geiger et al., Causal Abstractions, 2021.

Animation: a board with legal moves, a latent activation pushed along the mine-theirs direction, and a bar chart of next-move probabilities morphing after the internal board is edited.

13 · The synthesis

A map, not the territory.

Compressed, editable, plural, and sometimes wrong.

Nanda, 2023 · Elhage et al., Toy Models of Superposition, 2022 · Engels et al., 2025.

Animation: a floating holographic map above a dark mountain landscape; some tiles flicker and one beam reveals only a small patch of the terrain.
